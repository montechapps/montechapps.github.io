Open Tester Group

Join on Google Play
3

Install CalcQ

Once you have joined the test, use the Play Store link below to install the app on your phone.

Install from Google Play

Important: If the install page says the app is unavailable, you probably have not completed step 1 or step 2 yet, or you are signed into a different Google account.

If you see "Item not found": This can happen even after you complete steps 1 and 2. Google Play can take a little time to update closed testing access, so wait a few hours and try the install link again.

What I need from testers: Use the app over the next 14 days, try different calculators and tools, and report any bugs, crashes, or suggestions through Settings -> Report Issue.

Limited-time bonus: Premium is free to try during closed testing, so testers can access the full experience while helping improve the app.

Privacy & data safety

We built CalcQ to respect your privacy from the ground up.

  • No user accounts required — CalcQ does not directly collect or store personal information.
  • Ads are served through Google AdMob, which may use limited device identifiers for personalisation. You can opt out at any time in your device settings.
  • Crash reports are handled through Firebase Crashlytics to help us find and fix bugs quickly.
  • Full details are available in our Privacy Policy below.
