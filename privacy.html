Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

CalcQ does not require user accounts and does not directly collect or store personal information. Your calculations and history stay on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob). CalcQ uses Google AdMob to display advertisements. AdMob may collect limited device identifiers and usage data to provide and measure ads, including personalised ads where permitted. You can opt out of personalised ads in your device settings under Google → Ads.

Crash reporting (Firebase Crashlytics). CalcQ uses Firebase Crashlytics to collect crash reports. Crashlytics may collect crash diagnostics, device information, operating system version and app state information to help identify and fix software issues.

App analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase). CalcQ uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand general app usage, such as how often the app is opened, session length, app version and device type. Analytics may collect app usage events (including in-app purchase events), device information, approximate country and an app instance identifier. It does not record what you calculate.

Voice input (microphone). If you use voice input, CalcQ asks for microphone permission. Your speech is converted to text by your device's speech recognition service (for example, Google) and is used only to enter your calculation. CalcQ does not store or share voice recordings.

In-app purchases. Purchases are processed by Google Play. CalcQ does not receive or store your payment details.

Your privacy choices (EEA, UK and Switzerland). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, CalcQ asks for your consent before using your data for advertising and analytics. You can change your choices at any time in the app under Settings → Privacy Choices.

For more information about how Google handles data, see Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.
